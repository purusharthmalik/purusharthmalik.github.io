Research

Reinforcement learning for language models: how to make it cheaper, and the odd ways it changes a model. Full list on Google Scholar.

arXiv 2610.15371 / October 2026 / technical report

A Sovereign, Indic-First, Reasoning Model

S. S. Raj, P. Pasunuti, P. Chatterjee, D. Maan, P. Malik, N. Sarkar, A. Benki

Gnani-Evon-v3.3 is a 30B-parameter hybrid Mamba-Transformer mixture-of-experts model for reasoning and tool use in English and eight Indian languages, built from NVIDIA's Nemotron-3-Nano. The central finding is a side effect of reinforcement learning: rewards on verifiable tasks improved maths and science, but because they never checked the answer language, the share of Indic questions answered in English rose from 8.7% to 91.7%.
